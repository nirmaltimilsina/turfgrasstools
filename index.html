Using the calculator

Three steps to a clearer application schedule.

You do not need to enter weather records manually. The calculator can retrieve daily temperatures for your selected area, or you can use a nearby station or your own data.

01

Select turfgrass and PGR

Choose the turfgrass and plant growth regulator. The tool displays a reapplication interval only when a suitable published study is available.

02

Enter location and date

Search by city or ZIP code, adjust the map pin if needed, and enter the date of your last application.

03

View your results

Review GDD since application, daily temperatures, year-to-date GDD, and forecast progress toward the reapplication window.

Scientific basis

Weather data with research context.

Growing degree days summarize heat accumulation using daily high and low temperatures and a defined base temperature. A GDD threshold is not automatically transferable across species, PGRs, rates, or growing conditions.

The calculator distinguishes historical temperatures from forecast estimates and does not invent recommendations where evidence is insufficient.

Local temperature estimates

Temperature data estimated for the selected coordinates. Useful for broad coverage when a site-specific station is unavailable.

Nearby weather station

Recorded temperatures from a selected station. Actual conditions at the turfgrass site may differ, especially where stations are some distance away.

Published GDD intervals

Research-derived reapplication windows appear for supported turfgrass–PGR combinations. The calculator is a planning aid, not a substitute for the product label or turf response.
