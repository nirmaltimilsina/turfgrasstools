PGR Growing Degree Day Calculator

Calculate growing degree days (GDD) since a plant growth regulator application. Compare heat accumulation with published reapplication intervals where they are available, and review upcoming weather.

  • Historical and forecast temperatures
  • Nearby station or uploaded weather data
  • Published PGR intervals
Open PGR calculator

How it works

  1. Select your turfgrass and plant growth regulator.
  2. Choose a location and enter your last application date.
  3. Review accumulated GDD, weather data, and the application window if supported by research.

GDD estimates support application planning. Always consider the product label, rate, and turfgrass response.

Scientific basis

The PGR calculator uses temperature data to calculate GDD from a selected application date. It separates observed or historical values from forecast estimates.

Published thresholds depend on turfgrass species, PGR, rate, and conditions. The calculator does not provide a research-based interval for combinations without sufficient evidence.
